Wave Shooter

Every version

Each build is kept playable, so you can see how the game grew. Newest first. Co-op rooms only connect players on the same version.

  1. Latest · Version 7

    Holdout, weather and tactics

    Play

    Smoke grenades, flashbangs and firebombs, thrown with X. A helmet that stops shots to the head. Weather and time of day: dusk, night with a flashlight, rain and thick fog, or a mix that changes every three waves, and enemies see less in the dark and the fog. A settings screen for mouse sensitivity, field of view, volume and graphics quality. Faster armories: number keys buy, "Buy again" repeats your last order, and one button refills all your ammo.

  2. Version 6 · October 2026

    Holdout, real assets

    Play

    Real assets throughout. Photo-scanned stone, plaster, roof tiles, cobbles, wood and bark, under a photographed HDR sky that also lights the scene. Real crates, oil drums, fuel cans, ammo boxes and tyres, a real pistol model, and recorded gunfire, explosions and bullet impacts in place of synthesized ones.

  3. Version 5 · October 2026

    Holdout, real soldiers

    Play

    Enemies, the Delta squad and co-op players are now a real animated soldier model from Mixamo, with proper idle, walk and run animations, and hands that stay on the rifle while aiming. Each side wears its own colours.

  4. Version 4 · October 2026

    Holdout, realism update

    Play

    A big step towards realism. Photo-style stone, plaster, tiles and cobbles, a sky with clouds and a sun that lights and reflects off everything, bloom and film grading. Houses get proper windows, shutters and balconies, trees get leaves, cars look like cars. New weapon models with gloved hands, soldiers in camo with plate carriers and helmets, bullet holes, spent brass, weapon sway, settling recoil, echoing gunfire and footsteps.

  5. Version 3 · October 2026

    Holdout, sights and co-op fixes

    Play

    Open iron sights and red dot, a bigger sniper scope with a bold reticle, explosions that no longer stutter or slice through walls, and relay servers so co-op connects on mobile data.

  6. Version 2 · October 2026

    Holdout, first release

    Play

    MW3 Survival style. A sunlit village square, soldiers who shoot back, attack dogs, juggernauts and helicopters, three armories with air support and a Delta squad, plus 1–4 player co-op with voice chat.

  7. Version 1 · October 2026

    Bunker Nine Zombies

    Play

    Where it started: Black Ops style zombies in a boarded-up bunker, with a mystery box, perk machines and an upgrade station, plus co-op with voice chat.
